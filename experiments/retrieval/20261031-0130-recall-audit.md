Sample limits (shared GPU, ~1500 sessions per 8-15 min, two runs killed by other jobs): dev = 300 of the 398
answerable non-holdout questions (those whose histories were already embedded); dev-large = 30 questions
(multi-session, temporal, knowledge-update; picked for fewest new embeddings, 8.5k sessions embedded).
AGENTS.md on main now asks for all of dev minus holdout: the remaining 98 need ~3.7k more embeddings.

Metric: `sent_all` = all gold sessions inside the evidence `build_evidence` packs (18k chars), per type.

| type | n | sent_all before | after | n_sent before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 300 | 0.960 | **0.973** | 5.6 | 8.7 |
| multi-session | 71 | 0.930 | **0.972** | 5.3 | 10.3 |
| temporal-reasoning | 69 | 0.899 | **0.913** | 5.4 | 9.9 |
| knowledge-update | 43 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 4.3 | 7.7 |
| single-session-assistant | 86 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 6.6 | 7.6 |
| single-session-user | 31 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 5.3 | 7.0 |

R@k unchanged (session_ranking is by score). Cost: fewer characters from gold sessions in the pack (all types:
9.8k -> 7.2k chars; ss-assistant 6.0k -> 5.5k), because second/third chunks of the top session now compete with
first chunks of other sessions.

## Not adopted
- Reranker scores as logits (to make `min_score=-6` work as written): multi-session sent_all 0.930 -> 0.718.
- Diversifying every question: ss-assistant/ss-user lose 40-70% of gold-session characters for no recall gain.
- Boosting in-window rows inside the RRF fusion: pushed out-of-window gold out of the top 80 (dev-large temporal
  0.833 -> 0.750); replaced by adding the best 30 in-window chunks next to the top 80.
