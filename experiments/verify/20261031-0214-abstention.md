## Offline checks (no LLM)

- `coverage.py`: gold-session recall in the evidence, sample above: 4k 0.92, 8k 0.96, 12k 0.99, 18k 0.99
  (8k loses only multi-session 0.97→0.92 and temporal 0.98→0.90). A cutoff relative to the top reranker score

- `missing_terms.py` (all 423 non-holdout dev questions): `verify.missing_names` flags 6/25 abstention questions
  (Seattle, Peter, Sacramento, Tom, Italian, Shinjuku) and 2/398 answerable ones (Bachelor's/Computer Science,
  Evelyn Hugo). dev-large (~500 sessions): 2/25 vs 1/398. Lower-case words and word pairs were tried and dropped:
  they flag 90–100% of answerable questions.

## Findings

- The stand-in mostly writes `# FINAL ANSWER: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` at once, without reasoning. Asking to quote
  first and a smaller evidence (8k) together cut false abstention 0.52 → 0.20 and double evidence F1 (cite only
  quoted excerpts). Either alone helps little; the near-miss emphasis ("Check:" step) makes it much worse.
- Cost: abstention recall 0.76 → 0.52. In the replies the quotes show the swap plainly (guitar for violin,
  Harajuku for Shinjuku, baseball for football), but the 7B model answers anyway. With the name note 4/6 flagged
  questions abstain.
- On test (~6–8% unanswerable) final+v is a clear net gain (≈ +0.10 accuracy on answerable vs ≈ −0.02 from
  abstention); it does not meet "abstention recall kept" on this abstention-heavy sample.
